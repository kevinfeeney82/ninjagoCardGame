How to Play Cards
You can queue multiple cards at once and play them all together.
1
Click cards to add to queue (gold glow)
2
Dashed orbs show queued energy cost
3
Press Play Card to fire all queued cards
4
Draw (1 energy) adds a card from your deck
5
End Turn — enemy plays, shown in the battle feed
📚
Card Types
⚔️
Attack
Damage = card value + your Power stat.
🛡️
Defense
Adds shield that absorbs damage before HP.
📜
Support
Heal, draw cards, or set a dodge.
⚡
Special Mechanics
🛡️
Shield
Absorbs damage before HP. Excess goes through.
💨
Dodge
Next attack completely misses.
☠️
Poison
Ticks damage each turn. Stacks!
💉
Lifesteal (Nya)
Serpent Strike deals + poisons + steals 8 HP.
